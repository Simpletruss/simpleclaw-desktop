Tell your computer what to do.
SimpleClaw does it.

A desktop assistant for Windows, macOS, and Linux that carries out on-screen tasks for you. Describe a goal in plain language — SimpleClaw looks at your screen and drives the mouse and keyboard until it's done.

  • macOS Universal — Intel & Apple Silicon (.dmg)
  • Linux AppImage (.AppImage)

First launch differs per platform: macOS needs right-click → Open (the build isn’t signed yet) plus Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions, and Linux needs chmod +x and works best on X11. Per-platform setup →

⚠️ SimpleClaw takes real control of your mouse and keyboard. Start with Dry run on, try it on a throwaway window first, and keep your hand near the F9 emergency-stop key.

It works with whatever is on your screen — no per-app integrations, no scripting. Bring your own OpenAI-compatible vision model, and give it functions of your own with drop-in custom functions.

How it works

Every task runs as a loop of small, visible steps you can watch and stop.

You set a goal

Type what you want done and press Run.

It sees the screen

SimpleClaw captures the agent’s surface — a monitor, one window, or its own sealed browser.

The model decides

The AI picks the single next action.

It acts & repeats

The action runs, then the loop continues until finished.

Using it in four steps

First-time setup is one screen: point SimpleClaw at your AI model in ⚙ Settings (base URL, API key, model name).

  1. Type your goal in the goal bar — e.g. "Open Notepad and type hello world." Make sure the target window is open and visible.
  2. Keep Dry run ON and press Run. SimpleClaw shows what it would do without touching your mouse or keyboard.
  3. Watch the timeline & marker to confirm it's aiming at the right things.
  4. Turn Dry run OFF and Run again to let it act for real. Press F9 or Stop at any time.

What it can do each step

On every loop the model chooses exactly one action.

ActionWhat it does
clickSingle left-click at a point.
left_doubleDouble-click — e.g. to open an item.
right_singleRight-click for context menus.
dragPress, move, and release from one point to another.
hotkeyPress a keyboard shortcut (e.g. Ctrl+S).
typeType a string of text.
scrollScroll the view up or down.
waitPause briefly to let something load.
finishedDeclare the task complete and end the run.
